Crop: Rice
Optimal Soil pH: 5.5 to 6.5
Ideal Temperature: 20°C to 27°C
Water Requirement: High rainfall, typically above 200 mm.
Special Fact: Rice grows best in standing water and clayey soils.

Crop: Wheat
Optimal Soil pH: 6.0 to 7.0
Ideal Temperature: 15°C to 25°C
Water Requirement: Moderate rainfall, 75 to 100 mm.
Special Fact: Wheat is a rabi crop sown in winter and harvested in summer.
